1. Introduction

Huduma Platform is a multi-category service marketplace designed for Kenya and East Africa. It connects service seekers (people who need work done) with service providers (skilled professionals) across 19 service categories.

What You Can Do

Find Service ProvidersBrowse verified professionals for house help, plumbing, electrical work, tutoring, and more

Apply for WorkService providers can browse and apply to available jobs

3. Authentication (Login & OTP)

Huduma Platform uses phone-number-based authentication with OTP (One-Time Password) verification via SMS. No password is required.

Step 1: Onboarding

When you first open the app, you will see a three-page introduction:

Page 1 — Find Trusted Services: Learn about finding verified professionals near you.

Page 2 — Housing Made Easy: Discover rental and housing options.

Page 3 — Verified & Reviewed: Understand how reviews and verification work.

Swipe left/right to navigate between pages, then tap:

  • "Get Started" — To create a new account.
  • "I already have an account" — To sign in.

Step 2: Enter Your Phone Number

1 Your country code defaults to Kenya (+254).

2 Enter your 10-digit phone number (e.g., 0796XXXXXX).

3 Tap "Continue".

Step 3: Verify OTP

1 A 6-digit verification code will be sent to your phone via SMS.

2 Enter the code in the input field.

3 The code auto-submits when all 6 digits are entered.

Tip: If you did not receive the code, wait for the 60-second timer to expire, then tap "Resend Code". Ensure you have SMS reception.
Note: If this is your first time, you may be asked to set up your profile before proceeding.

5. Browsing Categories

How to Access

  • Tap the Categories tab in the bottom navigation bar

Category Cards

Each category is displayed as an elevated card with:

  • A colored icon representing the service type
  • The category name (e.g., "Plumber", "Electrician")

Tap any category card to view all listings in that category.

7. Creating a Listing

How to Create a Listing

1 Tap "Post a Job" on the Home Screen.

2 Fill in the listing form (see fields below).

3 Tap "Create Listing" to publish.

4 A success message appears and you return to the previous screen.

Listing Form Fields

FieldRequiredDescription
Listing TypeYesChoose "Hire Someone" or "Request Service"
TitleYesDescriptive title (minimum 10 characters)
CategoryYesSelect from the dropdown of 19 categories
DescriptionYesDetailed description (20–1,000 characters)
Budget MinNoMinimum budget in KES
Budget MaxNoMaximum budget in KES
Budget PeriodNoMonthly, weekly, daily, or one-time
LocationNoCounty or area where the work is needed
Engagement TypeNoOne-time, ongoing, or contract
Tips for a Good Listing:
  • Use a clear, specific title (e.g., "Need experienced plumber for kitchen sink repair in Westlands").
  • Write a detailed description including scope of work, requirements, and timeline.
  • Set a realistic budget range to attract qualified providers.
  • Specify the location to help providers assess commute distance.

8. Applying to a Listing

Tips: Write a personalized cover message highlighting your experience. Be responsive — check your messages after applying as the poster may reach out.

13. Reviews & Ratings

How Reviews Work

After a job is completed, both parties can leave reviews.

ComponentDescription
Rating1 to 5 stars
CommentWritten feedback about the experience

Leaving a Review

1 Navigate to the completed listing.

2 Tap the review option.

3 Select a star rating (1–5).

4 Write an optional comment.

5 Submit your review.

Note: Reviews are permanent and cannot be edited or deleted. Please be honest and constructive.

15. Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
OTP not receivedWait 60 seconds and tap "Resend Code". Check that your phone number is correct. Ensure you have SMS reception.
App won't loadCheck your internet connection. Try closing and reopening the app.
Cannot submit applicationEnsure you are logged in. Check that the listing is still active.
Profile not updatingEnsure all required fields (First Name, Last Name) are filled. Check your internet connection.
Messages not sendingCheck your internet connection. The app uses real-time WebSocket — a stable connection is required.
Listing not appearingNew listings may take a moment to appear. Pull down to refresh the listings page.
